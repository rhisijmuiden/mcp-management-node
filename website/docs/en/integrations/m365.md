# Microsoft 365 and Entra ID

Stackpilot reads each customer's Microsoft 365 tenant through Microsoft Graph: users, licences, admin roles, MFA,
risky users and the sign-ins that stand out. You can also block users, sign them out or reset their MFA or password.

## Connect a tenant, step by step

You do this once per customer, in the customer's own Entra ID.

1. In Stackpilot open **Environment → Microsoft 365** and click **Connect tenant** (or use the guide under
   Integrations). Choose the customer.
2. In the [Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com) of the customer, go to **App registrations → New
   registration**. Name it, for example, *Stackpilot*; single tenant is fine. Copy the **tenant id** and
   **application (client) id**.
3. Under **API permissions → Add a permission → Microsoft Graph → Application permissions**, add:
    - to read: `User.Read.All`, `Directory.Read.All`, `AuditLog.Read.All`, `UserAuthenticationMethod.Read.All`,
      `IdentityRiskyUser.Read.All`, `IdentityRiskEvent.Read.All`, `Policy.Read.All`, `Application.Read.All`;
    - for actions (optional): `User.EnableDisableAccount.All`, `User.RevokeSessions.All`,
      `UserAuthenticationMethod.ReadWrite.All`, `User-PasswordProfile.ReadWrite.All`, `IdentityRiskyUser.ReadWrite.All`.

    Then click **Grant admin consent**.
4. Choose how the app signs in:
    - a **client secret** (Certificates & secrets → New client secret), or
    - a **certificate**: Stackpilot can make one; download the `.cer` and upload it in Entra.
5. Paste the ids and the secret in Stackpilot and save. The secret or private key goes into the customer's
   [password vault](../customers/docs-vault.md#password-vault), never to the AI.

Stackpilot shows which parts it can read and, if not, why (a missing permission, or a tenant without Entra ID P1/P2).

## What you see

- **Tenants**: users, admins, users without MFA, risky users, security defaults, Conditional Access and when the app's
  secret expires.
- **Users**: licences, MFA, risk, last activity and status, with filters (admins, without MFA, risky, inactive,
  blocked but licensed, guests). Click a user for roles, MFA methods, notable sign-ins and risk detections.
- **Risk & MFA**: risky users, admins without MFA, risk detections of the last 7 days.
- **Sign-ins**: only those that stand out (risky, failed, from another country, old protocols), kept 30 days.
- **Licences**: bought, assigned and free per subscription.

## Actions

On a user: **sign out everywhere**, **block**, **unblock**, **reset MFA**, **reset the password** (a temporary password,
stored in the vault) and confirm or dismiss risk. Actions the app has no permission for are greyed out.

The AI can read everything with the scope `m365`. Actions (scope `m365_admin`) **always** wait for an admin's
approval.

## Alerts

Per tenant, each one can be switched off: risky users, admins without MFA, licence shortages, expired subscriptions,
the app secret expiring, many failed sign-ins and successful sign-ins from abroad. The morning summary adds a
Microsoft 365 category, with a proposal to block or sign out a risky user.
