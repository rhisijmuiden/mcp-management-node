# Monthly reports

Every customer can get an IT report per month, as a PDF in your house style and as HTML, written so that the customer
understands it.

## What is in it

- **Summary**: machines, availability, incidents, updates installed, backups, security points, AI changes and an
  overall verdict.
- **Work performed**, in plain language: every task on their machines (updates, backups, restarts, new servers,
  network changes, runbooks, automatic recovery, planned changes, handled tickets), with when, by whom (engineer, AI
  assistant, automatic or on request), how long it took and the result.
- **Availability** per server, **incidents** from monitoring, **updates** per machine, **backups**, **workstations** and
  their security status, **security** points, **AI actions** and **recommendations** from the optimisation scan.
- **Service availability (SLA)** from the [uptime checks](../care/monitoring.md#uptime-and-sla).
- Optionally the **cost statement** and what runs out soon.

## Mail it every month

1. Open the customer and the tab **Reports**.
2. Switch on **Monthly report**, choose the day (1–28), the language and the recipients (empty means the customer's
   main contact).
3. On that day the report of the previous month is made and mailed with the PDF and HTML attached.

## Make one by hand

**Make a report** for any month (the current month shows the days so far), with or without mailing it. View it,
download the PDF, mail it again or delete it.

The same tab holds the **maintenance report recipients** for the [Proxmox maintenance](maintenance.md#proxmox-maintenance)
timeline reports.
